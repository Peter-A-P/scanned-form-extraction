Forms That Read Themselves

Each way of reading a scanned form, measured on public pages with known answers: which fields it gets right, what it costs a thousand pages, and how it holds up on a bad scan. Every read on this site was made and graded beforehand; nothing is modelled in the browser and nothing is uploaded.

      The same numbers as the repository's README, from the same function. Intervals are 95% bootstrap over documents; each cost carries the date of the price it was computed at. FARA's registration forms name individuals, so they appear here as numbers and never as images.

      Pages shown: NIST Special Database 2 (synthesised 1988 tax forms), CORD v2 receipts (CC BY 4.0), and DeepForm ad-buy forms from Google's VRDU benchmark (FCC public files). Eight test pages per corpus, drawn by a fixed seed from sorted ids, never by how a reader did.
